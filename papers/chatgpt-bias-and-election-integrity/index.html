ChatGPT Bias and Election Integrity

Research Prologue: The significance of directional framing in AI-mediated political inquiry

Research edition: October 24, 2026 · Executive Rhetoric Ledger

Publication staging notice: This page is an editorial landing page for the supplied 55-page paper, not a substitute for its complete text. Research scope

The paper examines whether ChatGPT responses alter the framing of political inquiries through evidence refrain, query augmentation, proposition substitution, unrequested mitigating qualifications, asymmetric burdens of proof and related mechanisms. Its integrated manuscript reviews a 68-issue research inventory, while distinguishing issues from independent incidents.

Findings and limits

The manuscript identifies repeated question or proposition transformations across preserved and reconstructed interactions, including several assessed as favorable in framing effect to Trump-administration or Republican-associated actors. The paper does not claim a measured population-wide rate, deliberate provider intent or a demonstrated change in election outcomes. Repository assessments, original excerpts, external reporting and reconstructed interactions retain distinct evidence classifications.
