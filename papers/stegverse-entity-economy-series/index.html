The economic transition

Artificial intelligence does not only threaten existing professions. It can change who is able to participate in economic activity.

As capable intelligence becomes less expensive and more broadly available, services that were economically inaccessible to many people may become accessible to them. Productive capability that once depended on scarce professional labor can become more abundant. That can reduce scarcity premiums in some professions while enabling individuals and smaller organizations to undertake work, create products, solve problems, and form enterprises that were previously too expensive.

What may remain valuable

If expertise becomes less scarce, economic value does not disappear. It can move toward inputs and participation that remain differentiated: data, knowledge, experience, observation, context, evaluation, resources, relationships, and other attributable contributions.

The StegVerse proposition is that those contributions need not be silently extracted or surrendered. An entity can retain identity, authority, and custody while permitting defined uses of what it controls. Provenance can establish contribution. Permission can establish authorized use. Receipts can establish what occurred. Governed settlement can return attributable value to participating entities.

Series thesis

Abundant capability can expand who is able to create value, while sovereign control of attributable contributions can change who participates in the value that creation produces.

This does not attempt to preserve the scarcity structure of the old economy. It describes a possible transition toward a different one.

Formal foundation

The Entity Economy assumes that a participant can remain coherently identifiable across change and that authority, retained history, resources, purpose, recoverability, and relational standing are not silently collapsed during transitions. The deeper formal treatment is published in Coherent Life and Admissible Existence.

This series thesis is a public explanatory surface, not a new paper identity and not an empirical forecast. It does not claim that declining service costs, sovereign data control, attributable settlement, legal recognition, or broad economic redistribution are guaranteed outcomes. Those outcomes depend on implementation, adoption, measurement, enforceable authority, market behavior, and applicable law. Publication on Site grants no execution, transition, credential, custody, legal, or governance authority.
