StegVerse Cost and Capability Analysis

A lineage-backed relational test program covering historical reproduction, direct versus native batch routing, full versus StegVerse-managed context, fresh generation versus governed reconstruction, and repeated-trial reliability.

Decision

Do not approve a general StegVerse savings claim from the current evidence.

The program is decision-ready because it supports a bounded decision, not because it proves a favorable return on investment.

What the evidence establishes

Historical envelope reproducedThe retained baseline was reproduced within a narrow token and pricing-derived cost range, with an explicit reconstruction boundary.
Native batch route observedA real provider-native batch route was executed, but successful quality-equivalent savings were not established.
Managed context reduced input tokensIn the locked R3 workload, managed context reduced mean input tokens substantially, but neither lane produced successful outputs under the complete gate.
Reconstruction can avoid a new provider callFor already admitted work, governed reconstruction can avoid fresh external generation. It is a different operation, and net savings remain unproven.

R4 — Fresh generation versus governed reconstruction

Verdict: EXTERNAL_PROVIDER_GENERATION_CAN_BE_AVOIDED_FOR_ALREADY_ADMITTED_WORK_NET_SAVINGS_UNPROVEN.

Reconstruction of already admitted work can avoid a new external provider-generation charge. The retained avoidance scenarios were $0.03881 versus the OpenAI generation and $0.02295 versus the Anthropic generation.

Reconstruction is not equivalent fresh inference. Net savings and fully burdened ROI remain unproven because StegVerse compute, storage, verification, engineering, and maintenance costs have not been measured.

R5 — Reliability

The program retains 15 paired trials across R2 and R3. These relations estimate different causal effects and are not pooled into one savings percentage. Failures, retries, confidence intervals, and quality gates remain part of the result.

CFO findings

  • Historical control envelope reproduced: yes.
  • Successful route savings established: no.
  • Successful managed-context savings established: no.
  • External provider charge avoidance for admitted reconstruction established: yes.
  • Net reconstruction savings established: no.

Required before favorable ROI approval

  1. Measure StegVerse compute, storage, verification, engineering, and maintenance cost.
  2. Reconcile pricing-derived provider costs to invoices.
  3. Execute a successful quality-equivalent held-out context-management test.
  4. Calculate workload-weighted break-even and sensitivity ranges.
