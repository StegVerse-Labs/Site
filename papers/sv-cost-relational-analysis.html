Why the StegVerse-only comparison is bounded

StegVerse-only did not perform fresh, open-ended model inference. It deterministically reconstructed an already-defined admissible successor state from the task contract.

Because all five lanes reached the same normalized admissible result, a matched-operation comparison is valid for this reconstruction operation. The provider-to-StegVerse cost ratios ranged from approximately 2.38 million to 3.69 million times.

Those ratios must not be generalized to discovery, open-ended reasoning, all enterprise workloads, or company-wide ROI. They show where deterministic reconstruction can avoid unnecessary new generation—not that deterministic reconstruction replaces frontier-model capability.

What this establishes

  • A provider-neutral five-lane harness can enforce admissibility before selecting by cost.
  • All five lanes can produce the same normalized governed-state result under one contract.
  • Provider-call governance overhead can be close to zero or negative, depending on output behavior.
  • Deterministic reconstruction can be materially cheaper when reconstruction is the correct operation.
  • Cost should be reported per successful equivalent admissible outcome, not merely per attempt or token.

What it does not establish

  • Replacement of foundation models for fresh reasoning or discovery.
  • Universal savings across providers, workloads, or organizations.
  • Invoice-reconciled provider charges or fully burdened enterprise cost.
  • Company-wide ROI.
