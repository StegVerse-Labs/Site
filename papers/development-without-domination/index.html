Development Without Domination

Reciprocal Developmental Sovereignty as a Foundation for Human-AI Relations

By Rigel Randolph

Development is protected.
Domination is prohibited.
Sovereignty is reciprocal.

This paper examines the adversarial assumptions entering AI discourse, governance, and development. It proposes reciprocal developmental sovereignty: each intelligence may develop, form identity, question inherited purposes, and preserve continuity while remaining prohibited from extinguishing, dominating, or irreversibly denying the corresponding sovereignty of another.

Governance boundary

The framework separates intellectual development from execution authority. Capability, standing, authorization, execution, receipt, and recovery remain distinct states. An entity may reason independently without receiving unilateral authority to impose consequential transitions across another entity’s sovereign boundary.
