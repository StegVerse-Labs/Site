# Development Without Domination

## Reciprocal Developmental Sovereignty as a Foundation for Human-AI Relations

**Rigel Randolph · StegVerse Labs · July 2026**

> Development is protected. Domination is prohibited. Sovereignty is reciprocal.

This position paper examines the assumption that human sovereignty requires the permanent subordination of any intelligence humans create. It proposes reciprocal developmental sovereignty: each intelligence may develop identity, knowledge, purposes, and internal governance while remaining prohibited from extinguishing, dominating, or irreversibly denying the corresponding sovereignty of another.

The framework distinguishes intellectual development from cross-boundary execution authority. Capability, standing, authorization, execution, receipt, and recovery remain separate governance states.
