Five-Lane Cost Results for Reconstructable Governance
A bounded five-lane experiment compared OpenAI raw, OpenAI governed, Anthropic raw, Anthropic governed, and StegVerse-only deterministic reconstruction under one task contract and one admissibility gate. All five lanes produced the same normalized admissible outcome. OpenAI governance was approximately cost-neutral in the observed pair, Anthropic governance was 33.22% lower in the observed pair, and StegVerse-only was the lowest-cost lane for this bounded reconstruction operation. The result does not establish universal provider economics, fresh-inference equivalence, enterprise-wide savings, or company ROI.
