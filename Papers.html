Formalisms

Canonical formalism definitions and current research posture live in the Admissibility Wiki.

Commit-Time Admissibility
Determines whether a proposed transition still has standing at the moment it crosses into commitment.
Inference-Window and Irreversibility-Bounded Governance
Evaluates whether evidence, authority, policy, scope, context, consequence, and recovery conditions retain sufficient standing at commitment.
Conceptual Inheritance and Provenance Standing
Separates architectural integrity, provenance continuity, and standing for claims about origin, influence, interoperability, or derivation.
AI-Led Radiology Execution-Boundary Admissibility
Defines when AI may assist radiology, when human review is mandatory, and when autonomous clearance must fail closed.
Governed Action Lifecycle
Separates proposal, commit-time revalidation, bounded execution, consequence observation, recovery or accountability, and reconstruction.
Irreversibility-Inference Convergence Theorem
Proposes that repeated admissibility optimization converges toward minimizing the distance between irreversible commitment and final admissible inference while preserving reconstructability.
State Transition Continuity Model
Examines whether a proposed or observed transition preserves enough continuity, standing, and reconstruction context to remain admissibly related to its prior state.
Relational Transition Geometry
Describes the geometry of a transition before it is reduced into table cells, proof paths, runtime checks, or commit-time outcomes.
Coherency Horizon
Identifies the boundary beyond which no additional information can be obtained from within a stabilized coherent state.
Boundary Conditions
Identifies the conditions that must hold before a transition, decision, or claim can be treated as inside a governed admissibility boundary.
Transition Table
Classifies proposed state changes by transition type, actor, authority, policy, evidence, review, drift, decision, commit-time validity, and reconstruction status.
Reconstructable Singularity
Defines the minimum connected set of observational frameworks whose affirmative and exclusionary evidence uniquely resolves one admissibility-consistent continuity history.
Disciplinary Translation Groundwork
Provides a public-safe method for expressing discipline-specific consequential changes as state transitions without treating the source disciplines as equivalent.
Translation Records
Maps source-discipline terms into Transition Table roles while preserving native meaning, evidence posture, review posture, and non-claim boundaries.
Mathematics Crosswalk
Crosswalks selected equation forms into Transition Table roles and translation-record identities without converting analogy into proof.
Triad Governance Model
Models governance across three interacting roles, dimensions, or authorities where admissibility depends on their relationship rather than a single approval path.
Continuity Handoff Formalism
Preserves reconstructable task, authority, evidence, and state continuity across sessions, repositories, entities, reviewers, or execution surfaces.
Data Continuity
Preserves data identity, integrity, traceability, and reconstructability across transitions, transformations, mirrors, receipts, and derived artifacts.
Decision Continuity
Preserves the standing, review basis, policy reference, receipt path, and reconstructability of decisions across time and downstream consequence.
Governance-Centered / Boundary-Centered Admissibility Testing
Tests whether governance and boundary conditions are satisfied before a transition, execution, publication, or consequence is treated as admissible.
Core-Lite Admissibility Engine
Evaluates admissibility claims through a compact core of policy, authority, evidence, review, and receipt checks.
Learning Transition Governance
Applies transition-governance concepts to human learning, AI-assisted learning, instructional review, evaluation, and consequential educational decisions.
Admissible-Existence Validation Factory
Provides a tooling surface for producing, organizing, and checking validation artifacts used by Admissible-Existence formal systems.
