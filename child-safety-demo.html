Why the toggle is governance, not parental-control styling

The visible control tells the user which service profile is active. The authority remains underneath it. A protected child account cannot acquire stranger contact, public social exposure, precise location disclosure, public livestreaming, profiling, or engagement optimization merely by changing UI state. Normal Mode activation must be an admissible transition supported by age/eligibility evidence.
