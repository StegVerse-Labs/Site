Why this is different from ordinary parental controls

The protection is attached to the service authority, not merely hidden in a parent settings screen. A child cannot grant the service permission it does not possess. A parent cannot accidentally authorize unrelated advertising surveillance by approving a friendship. A platform cannot reinterpret permission to message a known friend as permission for strangers, precise location, public livestreaming, profiling, or engagement optimization.
