Why this matters as an alternative to a ban

A blanket ban treats access to the network as the problem. This model treats specific capabilities as the governed object. If stranger access, public discoverability, engagement optimization, behavioral profiling, unnecessary location disclosure, and similar risk-bearing capabilities are deterministically unavailable to a protected child while bounded communication and collaboration remain available, lawmakers can evaluate a technically enforceable middle path instead of choosing only between unrestricted social media and total exclusion.

2026-08-07 — Initial bounded-networking profile

Change: established Normal Mode / Child Mode distinction; protected Child Mode blocks stranger contact, public exposure, behavioral profiling, unnecessary precise-location disclosure, public self-livestreaming under the protective minor policy, and engagement-optimized recommendation while preserving known-person communication, approved collaboration, and report/block capability.
