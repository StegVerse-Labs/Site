TIDC Reliability Workflow

This page publishes the independent-coding and disagreement-preservation process for Technology-Induced Discovery Clustering. Reliability asks whether independent coders can reproduce classifications from the evidence. It does not ask whether the hypothesis appears supported.

Authority boundaries

Snapshot ≠ independent coding

The first-pass snapshot records prior classifications for comparison only.

Agreement ≠ confirmation

High agreement would indicate coding reproducibility, not proof of clustering.

Resolution ≠ erasure

Resolved disagreements remain in the historical record with rationale and sources.

Site ≠ proof authority

This page is a public research mirror and workflow surface.
