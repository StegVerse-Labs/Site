Add the email addresses that belong in your personal profile. Each address can stay as contact information only or be connected independently through the governed email-continuity flow.

Credential boundary: this page never asks for or stores mailbox passwords, OAuth tokens, app passwords, recovery codes, or other provider secrets. After a mailbox is mapped, credential setup belongs in SKAP Vault.

Personal Information

Email addresses are ordinary profile data until you explicitly choose Connect this email. A primary address is only your display/contact preference.

How connection works

  1. Add one or more addresses to Personal Information.
  2. Choose Connect this email only for the address you want StegVerse to map.
  3. The canonical KV email bridge resolves the provider and creates the bounded mailbox mapping.
  4. When mapping succeeds, this page directs you to complete credential setup in SKAP Vault.
  5. Provider verification and governed mail admission remain separate steps.

If the canonical KV bridge is unavailable, connection fails closed and the address remains ordinary profile information.
