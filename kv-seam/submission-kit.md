## The 4 steps

1. ESTABLISH YOUR NODE — a first submission stating who you are
   and where, in your own storage, returns should be sent. Its
   response (SUCCESS, unless something is unsupported) equips
   you and confirms your node as healthy. Done once.
2. BUILD YOUR MANIFEST — fill in the scaffold below. The
   automated builder can draft it from your stated purpose;
   you confirm.
3. SUBMIT — deliver the manifest and keep your packet
   reachable at its locator. The seam's inbox is a folder in
   a Google Drive owned by stegverse@gmail.com. Three ways in:
   (a) deposit the manifest at the inbox link —
   https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1kZcD-BoKTl_b79iwH5pgNfE8c8U9QgDh
   (depositing asks you to be signed in to any Google
   account; the link is a standing approval to deposit, and
   depositors can see one another's manifests there);
   (b) share it with stegverse@gmail.com from your own Google
   Drive; (c) paste it in Ecosystem Chat. If your files live
   on another cloud service, they can stay there — only the
   manifest travels; your packet is read at the link your
   manifest names. The deposit itself is the signal that
   starts the work; no email is used by the process. Only
   manifests are worked — anything else deposited is held,
   unprocessed. The request itself is the work order, receipted
   start to finish. Submissions are worked by the ecosystem's
   lanes in their working sessions, in claim order — expect a
   working-session turnaround, not an instant reply.
4. OPEN YOUR RESULT FILE — at submission you are answered
   with ONE link: your result file, named for the receipt
   that pins it (RESULT__<receipt>.md). The file is a
   projection of your run: it displays the replay of its
   receipt segment from that pinned receipt to the current
   location in the process — the journey (every receipted
   step) and the governance (every disposition) as they
   stand. Open or refresh it and the replay is where the
   run is: received, governed, returned. COMPLETE is not a
   separate document — it is the final state transition in
   the same replay, at the same link. Its header carries
   STATE (the last transition) and FINAL: NO/YES for
   machines polling the link. (A copy is also deposited as
   the return of record.)

## More than one landing — projection

Name a PROJECTION SCOPE in your manifest: SUBMITTER-ONLY (the
default), NAMED locations, or OPEN. Anyone inside the scope
can request the result at their own storage location — the
same bytes, byte-identical everywhere, each landing verifiable
against the receipt. One run, seen by everyone you name,
instead of the same run repeated for each reader.

## Asking again — replay and reconstruction

Anyone holding a result's receipt can submit a new manifest
naming that receipt to request a REPLAY (the run re-executed
from its recorded inputs; the result declares both receipts
and whether the dispositions are identical) or a
RECONSTRUCTION (the run's record reassembled from the ledger
alone, every link verified).

## The rules

No credentials — anything asking for one is not this seam.
Custody never moves: the original stays with you, a changed
file is a mismatch, not an arrival. Zero user data is
collected; the seam holds receipts, not submissions. A
governed response, a HOLD, or silence are the only outcomes;
anything else is not from here.
