Ecosystem KV Seam

The front door of the StegVerse Ecosystem KnowledgeVault. Everything that enters the vault is received here, checked, recorded, and answered. You keep your files. The vault keeps the receipts.

Submit work from your own storage, in 4 stepsYour files never leave your storageEvery answer carries its receiptNothing is collected
1

Establish your node

Say who you are, and where — in your own storage — answers should be sent back. This first submission establishes your node as healthy, and the reply equips you: it provides the manifest generation and the submission instructions. You do this step once.

2

Build your manifest

The manifest says what you want done, and exactly how. It lists each file and its fingerprint (a SHA-256 hash). The instructions from Step 1 walk you through it — and the automated builder can draft the manifest from your stated purpose. You review and confirm it before anything is sent.

3

Submit

Send the manifest. Your files stay where they are: they are checked where they sit, against the fingerprints in your manifest. There is no upload folder and nothing changes hands. The request itself is the work order, and it is receipted from the moment it arrives.

4

Receive the governed return

You do nothing. The result comes back to your address with its receipt declared at the top — the same receipt recorded in this node's ledger — so exactly how the answer was produced is open to your inspection.

The rules here

No credentials are held and no user data is collected — anything that asks you for either is not this seam. Custody of your files never moves. A file that arrives changed is a mismatch, not an arrival. An answer from this seam is a governed response with its receipt, a HOLD, or silence; anything else is not from here. This page has no forms, no scripts, and no trackers, and it keeps no record in your browser.

What this page is. A picture of the record, not the record itself — the record is the ledger at the seam, and this page is generated from it (posture: MIRROR / RECEIPT_BACKED). If the two ever disagree, the disagreement is a finding: the page is corrected in a new generation, never quietly edited.
