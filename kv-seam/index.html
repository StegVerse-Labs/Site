How to proceed

1. Start with the bootstrap. The submitter bootstrap (SUBMITTER__BOOTSTRAP__01 · SHA-256 583c8f0450717af998eab1925b020a6697831d6a2aa9c652fd1655971cda2e11, held at the seam) is the minimal instruction: the address, the conformance probe, the response grammar. It is one text for a human submitter and a machine submitter alike.

2. Your first submission is a conformance test. A minimal probe — who you are, your return address in your own storage, the capability you request; no files. Expect a SUCCESS disposition (LLM Adapter Protocol generation 03 · SHA-256 940ff97f09d882a673be27f7eadb3295d793893a8772dc060274e41195535a1d). An unsupported request is named in the disposition. Anything else is an error or failure state, whose only legitimate forms are SILENCE or HOLD — any other answer is not from this seam.

3. Submit from your own storage. Included in the SUCCESS disposition is the current instruction for the Manifest and File submission (at this generation: SUBMISSION__INSTRUCTION__02 · SHA-256 d9e8c306bef76d799908be080a00c59cb7bdd501e3c53d7a754e32534a4060f8, with addenda). Prepare a projection of each file — the original stays in your storage; custody never moves — and a manifest that discusses the precise protocol of the evaluation you request, with the SHA-256 of every original and every projection. There is no landing folder: ingress verifies your bytes as they sit against your manifest's hashes and receipts the anchors. Your submission request is queue work, receipted start to finish, and it incurs a return file at your address: the results and the complete receipt chain segment of your submission, the receipt declared at the top of the file.
