The rules here

No credentials are held and no user data is collected — anything that asks you for either is not this seam. Custody of your files never moves. A file that arrives changed is a mismatch, not an arrival. An answer from this seam is a governed response with its receipt, a HOLD, or silence; anything else is not from here. Every button on this page acts only on your own device — registering it, opening projections, replaying the ledger card, sharing, and copying. This page has no forms and no trackers and collects nothing. Its scripts run on your device: the ledger card's handlers, and the Site's StegOS node stack behind the register button — which keeps your device node's record in your browser, as that node's design intends. The seam's own record is never in your browser; it is the ledger at the seam.

What this page is. A picture of the record, not the record itself — the record is the ledger at the seam, and this page is generated from it (posture: MIRROR / RECEIPT_BACKED). If the two ever disagree, the disagreement is a finding: the page is corrected in a new generation, never quietly edited.
