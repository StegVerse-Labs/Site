What this adds

Economic state, not one index

The report contract can preserve nominal prices, physical quantity or service state, mandatory costs, producer cost/margin surfaces, household burden, unmet need, distributional divergence, uncertainty, and methodology breaks as separate evidence attributes.

What this does not do

No browser-side economic authority

The page does not calculate an inflation substitute, select evidence on its own, bridge incompatible methodologies, reconcile conflicting sources, or turn a proxy into a direct measure. Those transitions remain upstream and receipted.
