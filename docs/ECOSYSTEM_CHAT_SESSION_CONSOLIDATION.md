## Active claims

The machine-readable inventory is authoritative. Claims must include creation time, expiration or release condition, expected evidence, collision boundaries, and next action.

A claim is stale when its expiration passes without matching evidence. Stale claims resolve to `BLOCKED` or are released to `UNCLAIMED`; they do not imply success.
