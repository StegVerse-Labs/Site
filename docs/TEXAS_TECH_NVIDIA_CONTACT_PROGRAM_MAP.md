Verified access boundary:

1. generally available resources are available to TTU faculty, research grant leaders, students working with them, and—by special request—external research partners of TTU faculty;
2. an external research partner requires a TTU faculty/staff sponsor;

## Recommended routing sequence

1. Send the bounded research concept to Research & Innovation Partnerships and request routing to a faculty or research owner working in AI systems, cybersecurity, distributed systems, or research computing.
2. Send HPCC a technical inquiry asking whether the minimal experiment and scaling plan fit existing resources and what sponsor/authorization path applies.
3. Use Research Commercialization only when the conversation includes IP, licensing, startup participation, or a formal industry partnership.
4. Do not request credentials, allocation, priority access, or institutional marks before a TTU sponsor and authorized process are identified.
