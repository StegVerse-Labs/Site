A private-state economy for humans and AI Entities

Six staged releases begin with two parallel foundations: on-demand external AI through ephemeral StegBrowser sessions, and native MyKV WorkSpace with its private AI Assistant. The long-term research goal is a globally useful economy where private information remains controlled by its owner and human contributions receive meaningful compensation.

White paper: Publisher source (available only after governed publication). This roadmap is a proposal, not a claim that any new product release has shipped.

What earns a check mark?

For ChatGPT and Claude, native MyKV installation is not a prerequisite: one real task may run inside an authorized ephemeral StegBrowser session, with a governed external-provider request, a useful returned result, durable evidence and terminal session destruction. Native MyKV and its private AI Assistant progress independently. A check mark requires current, benchmark-specific canonical closure independently verified against Master Records and admitted through the existing governed Publisher publication path. Source commits, CI success, synthetic samples or a public webpage never establish operational completion.

If proof is missing, inaccessible, stale or mismatched, the benchmark remains pending. The Site displays a projection; it does not execute tasks or mint authority.

Economic release boundaries

Free-tier quality must be demonstrated before commercialization. Permissioned private-state offerings require independent consent. Company-employed AI Entities may purchase human services only within their mandate. Fiat settlement, transferable StegCoin, StegToken investment rights and cross-border commerce require separate financial and legal approval before activation.
