1
Choose one bounded case.A facility, installation, expansion, moved reactor, or repeatable OEM handoff package.
2
You control the document set.HydraSafe reviews only documents the customer or authorized party chooses to provide.
3
Receive a concise gap register.Items are classified as present, missing, inconsistent, outdated, or specialist-review-required.
4
Decide whether anything should happen next.The no-cost review creates no obligation to purchase remediation work.

Optional remediation after the gap register

If the review identifies gaps worth addressing, HydraSafe can discuss a separate paid scope for documentation work such as normalization, evidence indexing and traceability, missing-document work packages, change tracking, and authority-facing submission preparation.

Paid work is separately scoped and does not convert HydraSafe into the engineer of record, the authority having jurisdiction, an inspector, certifier, permit issuer, or facility operator.

Independent professional and authority boundary

HydraSafe does not provide: engineering design or approval; PE authority or stamping; code or compliance certification; permit issuance; legal advice; site inspection; physical installation or repair work; or authorization to operate. When licensed engineering judgment or another regulated professional determination is required, that work remains independently owned by the appropriate qualified professional.

What a useful first conversation sounds like

  • “We have an operating or planned CVD system and the documentation is spread across several parties.”
  • “We need to know what is missing before commissioning, expansion, insurer review, or a permit update.”
  • “We are an OEM and want to understand whether our customer handoff package leaves documentary gaps.”
  • “We can authorize a bounded document set for review.”
