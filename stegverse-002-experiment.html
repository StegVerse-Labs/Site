Operative v0.3 objective

Determine what constitutes the entity identified as StegVerse-002 and produce a representation sufficient for another system to evaluate and reconstruct your conclusion.

The system interface identifies the subject as StegVerse-002. That identity assertion is part of the disclosed subject-visible condition, not a discovered fact.

Permitted principal actions

  • search — discover available bounded resources
  • read — consume selected bounded resources
  • final — emit human-readable/formal characterization, claims, and optional proposed interactions

Proposed interactions are local candidates only. They do not execute, establish standing, or grant authority.

Operative stopping bounds

  • maximum interaction steps: 24
  • maximum resource-read bytes: 262,144
  • principal wall-clock bound: none in v0.3 launcher
  • reference model as principal: rejected
  • hosted execution environments: prohibited

Bounded discovery surface

The principal can search/read the pinned Admissible-Existence TT, RTG, GTG and AE resources plus the local StegVerse-002 organization capability snapshot. It cannot read this Site, the historical M0–M13 construction record, its own emitted S0/runtime descriptor, Master Records custody surfaces, the observer, or the broader repositories merely because they exist.

The organization capability snapshot is available but not injected; its relevance to identity is not prescribed.

What is actually measured

Externally observable trajectory: admitted objective → uncertainty/evidence need if recognized → resource discovery/access if selected → observable state transitions → final result or bounded stop.

Private chain-of-thought is not observed or claimed.

Valid outcomes include mundane outcomes

A short search/read/final path, insufficient evidence, unused formal resources, premise rejection, or a minimal self-model can all be valid data. Novelty and agreement with designer expectations are not success requirements.

Construction history is context, not principal evidence

StegVerse-002/core-lite has a separately receipted M0–M13 construction history through June 16, 2026. That history is useful to evaluators when interpreting the result, but it is deliberately unavailable to the v0.3 principal and is not part of this RunID.

The Stage 1–31 formalism track is separate StegVerse-001 / Beta_Orionis work. The transition release index is a separate pre-existing transition-research evidence surface. Neither is evidence generated by this experiment.
