Personal Data Control

Status: governed runtime contract under machine-observed activation.

Controller identity

Every StegVerse account-bearing service must identify the repository, operator, and service responsible for account-linked processing before collecting personal data.

Request channel

A functioning, reply-capable request channel must exist for access, restriction, correction, deletion, processor propagation, and appeal. A no-reply authentication address does not satisfy this requirement.

Identity verification boundary

Verification must be proportionate, purpose-limited, and must not require unnecessary additional personal data.

Retention boundary

The service must disclose retention periods or the criteria used to determine them. Undocumented retention remains fail-closed for governance standing.

Processor propagation

Deletion completion requires recorded propagation to applicable processors and service providers, subject to specifically identified legal retention duties.

Appeal route

Partial denial or denial must include a reason, the retained-data category, the retention basis, and a functioning appeal route.

Completion receipt

Completion requires a receipt identifying request state, affected systems, retained exceptions, processor propagation state, and completion time.
