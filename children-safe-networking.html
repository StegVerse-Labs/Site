Governed Child-Safe Networking

Status: public governance and communications contract under repository-native machine validation.

Try it: Open the live child-safety governance demo to test age bands, jurisdictions, known contacts, stranger contact, public posting, livestreaming, location, profiling, recommendation systems, and inspectable governance receipts.

Core principle

Governance does not have to keep children off the network. It can make the network more worthy of children by restricting unnecessary collection, limiting authority, and preserving safe participation.

Privacy-first defaults

  • Data harvesting is optional: useful baseline service must not depend on unrelated behavioral tracking, cross-service profiling, contact harvesting, or advertising surveillance.
  • Data minimization: collect only information required for the declared service purpose.
  • Purpose limitation: data collected for one purpose must not silently become training, advertising, profiling, or unrelated decision data.
  • Location hidden by default: precise location disclosure requires an explicit governed purpose and permission.
  • No advertising profile by default: child participation must not require creation of a behavioral advertising dossier.
  • Bounded retention: retained information must have a declared retention boundary and deletion path.

Safe networking without isolation

Children should still be able to socialize, learn, create, play, share music, and build friendships. Governance should constrain the data and authority surrounding those activities rather than treating disconnection as the only safety model.

Governed permissions

Identity, contacts, camera, microphone, location, files, and cross-service data each require separate purpose-bound authority. Permission to use one capability does not imply permission to use another.

Parent and child visibility

Protection should be understandable. A governed surface should be able to show states such as Private, Approved Friends, Location Hidden, and No Advertising Profile without exposing hidden surveillance controls as a substitute for safety.

Governance boundary

Publication of this page is a communications and policy projection only. The interactive demo proves deterministic browser-level policy behavior, not production age assurance, parental authorization, identity verification, runtime enforcement, deletion completion, legal compliance, or production activation. Those states require their own executable evidence and receipts.
