### technology_dependency

- `Necessary`: the result could not reasonably have been produced in the reported form without the technology.
- `Material`: the technology substantially changed feasibility, speed, scale, collaboration, or verification, but necessity is not established.
- `Supportive`: the technology assisted the work but was not central.
- `Incidental`: the technology was present but not meaningfully causal.
- `Unresolved`: supplied evidence is insufficient.

### orientation

- `Self-capability`: the principal object of study is the technology's own construction, behavior, limits, benchmarking, control, or verification.
- `External`: the technology is principally used to solve or advance a problem outside its own capability.
- `Mixed`: both are materially present and neither is clearly secondary.
- `Unresolved`: supplied evidence is insufficient.

### problem_origin_type

- `Inherited problem`: the problem materially predates the technology application.
- `Newly formulated by technology`: the problem exists principally because the technology or its new capabilities exist.
- `Mixed`: inherited and technology-created problem elements are both material.
- `Unresolved`: supplied evidence is insufficient.

### coding_confidence

- `High`: classification is directly supported by the supplied evidence.
- `Medium`: classification is reasonable but one or more important details remain indirect or incomplete.
- `Low`: classification is tentative.
- `Insufficient`: no defensible classification can be made.
