Create a VA disability compensation claim
with the VA app, VA.gov, and ChatGPT.

This page gives a linear process for downloading your VA medical record, organizing claim evidence, drafting truthful statements, and filing through VA.gov. ChatGPT is used as an evidence organizer and drafting assistant—not as a clinician, accredited representative, or VA decision-maker.

Begin the guideOpen the official VA application
Important: Never submit invented facts, altered dates, unsupported diagnoses, or a medical nexus that no qualified clinician provided. A diagnosis by itself does not prove service connection.
Protect your information: A Blue Button report can contain your Social Security number, address, diagnoses, medications, and other sensitive records. Use a private device, trusted network, secure storage, and a private ChatGPT conversation. Keep an untouched original of every downloaded record.

Phase 1 — Download your complete VA medical record

1Open the official VA app

Sign in. On the Home screen, tap Health. The annotated image shows the first navigation target.

2Open Medical records

From the Health screen, tap Medical records.

3Continue to VA.gov

Tap Review medical records on VA.gov. Sign in through ID.me or Login.gov when prompted and authorize VA.gov to receive the identity information needed for the session.

4Open the download-report workflow

On the Medical records page, find Download your medical records, then choose Go to download your medical records reports.

5Start a Blue Button report

Under Download your VA Blue Button report, choose Select records and download report.

6Select the date range

Choose All time unless you have a specific reason to limit the record. Continue to the record-type screen.

7Select the available record categories

Choose Select all VA records. Confirm that labs and tests, care summaries and notes, vaccines, allergies and reactions, health conditions, vitals, medications, and any other displayed categories remain checked.

8Choose PDF and download

Choose PDF for a readable report. Tap Download report. When your browser displays a second download prompt, tap Download again. VA.gov's green “download has started” message does not necessarily mean the browser prompt was completed.

9Find and preserve the file

Open the iPhone Files app. Check Recents first, then Browse. Some browsers save the file in a browser-named folder rather than the folder labeled Downloads. Rename the file clearly, such as VA-Blue-Button-All-Time-2026-07-31.pdf.

Phase 2 — Upload the report to ChatGPT

10Attach the PDF

Open a new private ChatGPT conversation. Tap the + button, choose the file option, and select the Blue Button PDF from Files.

11Run a bounded evidence review

Use the following prompt. Require page or section references, separation of facts from inference, and explicit identification of missing evidence.

Review the attached VA Blue Button report as an evidence organizer, not as a decision-maker. Create a table with one row per potentially claim-relevant condition. For each condition list: exact documented diagnosis or symptom wording; earliest documented date; later treatment dates; medications or procedures; objective tests; functional limitations recorded by clinicians; any statement connecting the condition to military service or another condition; and the exact page or section supporting each entry. Separate facts stated in the record from inferences. Do not invent a diagnosis, service event, onset date, nexus, or severity. Mark missing evidence clearly.

Phase 3 — Build one claim theory at a time

Direct service connectionCurrent disability or persistent symptoms, an in-service event/injury/disease, and evidence connecting the two.
Secondary service connectionA new condition caused or aggravated by an already service-connected disability or its treatment.
IncreaseAn already service-connected disability that has worsened, supported by current medical or lay evidence.
Presumptive or exposure-basedA condition and service circumstance covered by current VA eligibility rules. Verify the official rule before relying on this path.
AggravationEvidence that service or a service-connected condition measurably worsened another condition beyond its expected course.

Run a gap analysis

Using only the attached report and the worksheet you created, classify every proposed condition as:
A. Ready for factual drafting;
B. Needs service-event documentation;
C. Needs current diagnosis or updated severity evidence;
D. Needs a qualified medical nexus or aggravation opinion;
E. Needs lay or witness evidence;
F. Appears duplicative, speculative, or unsupported.
For each classification, explain the missing item and the safest next action. Do not state that a claim is fully verified merely because a diagnosis exists.

Phase 4 — Draft the personal statement

Draft a first-person Statement in Support of Claim for [CONDITION]. Use only facts I confirm. Organize it as:
1. Condition claimed;
2. In-service event, injury, illness, or exposure;
3. When symptoms began;
4. Continuity and treatment history;
5. Current symptoms and frequency;
6. Functional effects on work, sleep, mobility, concentration, relationships, and daily activities;
7. Evidence attached.
Use plain language. Do not add medical conclusions or claim that a clinician gave a nexus opinion unless the record says so. Put [VERIFY] beside every date or fact that requires my confirmation.
  • Confirm the exact condition name and whether it is left, right, or bilateral.
  • Confirm dates, locations, units, deployments, injuries, exposures, and witnesses.
  • Describe frequency, duration, severity, flare-ups, and functional effects truthfully.
  • Do not convert your belief into a clinician's medical opinion.
  • Attach service records, private records, orders, incident records, buddy statements, and prior decisions that support the theory.

Phase 5 — Organize and quality-check the packet

  1. Create a separate folder for each claimed condition.
  2. Keep the untouched Blue Button report in a source-records folder.
  3. Create an evidence index listing each file, date range, source, and condition supported.
  4. Use descriptive names such as 01-Personal-Statement-Right-Hip.pdf and 02-Service-Treatment-Record-Hip-Injury.pdf.
  5. Compare every drafted sentence against the original record and your personal knowledge.
  6. Remove unsupported conclusions, duplicate material, and contradictory dates.
  7. Have a clinician address diagnosis, causation, aggravation, and severity when medical expertise is required.

Phase 6 — File on VA.gov

  1. Open the official online disability compensation application, VA Form 21-526EZ.
  2. Starting the verified online disability application automatically notifies VA of an intent to file for that application; VA says a separate intent-to-file form is not needed for an online disability claim.
  3. Confirm personal, contact, banking, and service information.
  4. Add each disability using a precise condition name.
  5. Identify VA and private treatment sources completely.
  6. Upload the organized supporting files and use your evidence index to check completeness.
  7. Review every answer before certification, then sign and submit.
  8. Save the confirmation number and submitted-claim summary.
  9. Monitor claim status and evidence requests through VA.gov or the VA app and preserve proof of each response.

When to use accredited assistance

Use an accredited Veterans Service Organization representative, claims agent, or attorney when records are missing, a prior claim was denied, the effective date matters, service events are disputed, conditions overlap, or the theory depends on complex secondary causation or aggravation.
