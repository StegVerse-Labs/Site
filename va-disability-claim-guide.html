2

Create your VA.gov sign-in account

  1. Tap Open VA.gov Sign In below.
  2. Choose the sign-in method you prefer: Login.gov or ID.me.
  3. If you do not already have that account, choose Create account and follow that provider’s instructions.
  4. Use the email address from Step 1. Watch for the confirming email, open it, and follow its link or instructions to finish creating the account.
  5. Return to VA.gov and sign in successfully with the account you created.

3

Open the VA medical-record download page

Use the browser link below, or use the VA Health and Benefits app.

Open VA medical-record download page

Browser route: Open the link above and sign in with the Login.gov or ID.me account you created.

App route: Download or open the official VA: Health and Benefits app. Sign in with Login.gov or ID.me, open Health, then Medical records. If the app shows Review medical records on VA.gov, tap it, then choose the option that takes you to download your medical-record reports.

Get the VA Health and Benefits app

App wording can change. If you cannot find the expected control, use the direct VA.gov browser link above.

4

Download all available medical records

On the Blue Button / download page:

  1. Under Select the records you want to download, set Date range to All time.
  2. Set Types of records to include to All.
  3. For file type, choose PDF or TXT. Either is fine for this workflow.
  4. Tap or click Download report.

5

Find the file on your phone

Make sure you can actually find the downloaded file before continuing.

If you used the browser, look in your phone’s Downloads folder. On iPhone, this is usually in the Files app. On Android, look in Files or Downloads.

If you used the VA app and cannot find the downloaded report: return to the direct VA.gov browser link in Step 3 and download it again through the browser. Do not continue until you can see the actual PDF or TXT file on your device.

6

Submit the file to VA Claims Chat

Open VA Claims Chat and follow its document instructions.

Open VA Claims Chat
Current safety gate: only upload the medical-record file if VA Claims Chat presents an active secure document-upload control. If the page says private document upload is not active, do not paste medical records into the text box. Follow the page’s instructions and stop at that boundary.
