The Guide and Claims Chat may explain procedures, identify evidence categories, organize user-confirmed facts, preserve uncertainty, and point to official sources. They do not adjudicate, provide medical opinions, guarantee ratings, act as an accredited representative, sign forms, or submit claims.

Submission authority remains with the veteran

Automated filing does not mean autonomous filing from uploaded records. Before any submission, the veteran must confirm every material fact, select every claimed condition, review the exact package, provide the required signature or authorization, and approve the final submission through an authorized VA or accredited-representative integration.

Phase 1 — Preserve and identify records

1Download official records

Use the VA app and VA.gov to obtain the complete available medical record, including a Blue Button report when applicable. Preserve an untouched original.

2Gather all claim-relevant evidence

Include supportive, unfavorable, conflicting, and incomplete evidence: service records, private and VA medical records, prior decisions, examinations, opinions, witness statements, employment records, symptom logs, exposure records, and other relevant material.

3Protect sensitive information

Records may contain Social Security numbers, addresses, diagnoses, financial information, witness identities, and other sensitive data. Use trusted devices and networks, preserve originals, and disclose only what is necessary.

Phase 2 — Build a governed evidence map

Until native private document upload is activated, use the Claims Chat for public-source guidance and organize records locally. The future private workspace will perform the following under privacy, custody, and reconstruction controls:

For each proposed condition, build a claim-evidence table that separates:
1. Current diagnosis or persistent symptoms;
2. In-service event, injury, illness, exposure, or onset evidence;
3. Continuity and treatment history;
4. Nexus, secondary causation, or aggravation evidence stated by qualified sources;
5. Current severity and functional effects;
6. Lay and witness evidence;
7. Contrary, inconsistent, or potentially unfavorable evidence;
8. Missing evidence and unresolved uncertainty.

Every factual entry must identify its source document and page or section. Facts, user statements, source conclusions, assistant inference, and uncertainty must remain distinct.

Phase 3 — Establish the claim theory

  • Direct: current disability, in-service event or disease, and competent evidence connecting them.
  • Secondary: a condition caused or aggravated by an already service-connected disability or treatment.
  • Increase: evidence that an already service-connected condition has worsened.
  • Presumptive or exposure: current official eligibility requirements and qualifying service.
  • Aggravation: evidence of measurable worsening beyond the expected course.

The system must identify what is supported, what conflicts, what is missing, and what requires a clinician, accredited representative, or other qualified human.

Phase 4 — Prepare veteran-review materials

  • One evidence index per claimed condition.
  • A dated timeline with source and page references.
  • Draft statements limited to veteran-confirmed or source-supported facts.
  • Contradictions, unfavorable evidence, and uncertainty shown rather than hidden.
  • A missing-evidence and next-action list.
  • A change log showing what the system drafted, revised, or flagged.

Phase 5 — Filing gates

  1. Identity and account authority verified.
  2. Veteran consent to process the admitted records.
  3. Every material fact reviewed and confirmed.
  4. Every claimed condition selected by the veteran.
  5. Current form, rule, and evidence requirements verified.
  6. Contradictions and unresolved items displayed.
  7. Exact package hash generated.
  8. Veteran authorizes that exact package.
  9. Signature or legally valid delegation verified.
  10. Authorized VA or accredited-representative transport available.
  11. Duplicate, retry, revocation, and partial-failure controls pass.
  12. Submission confirmation, custody, and reconstruction receipts persist.

Until every applicable gate verifies, the system may prepare and review materials but must not submit the claim.

Official starting points

  • VA Form 21-526EZ
  • How to file a disability claim
  • Evidence needed
  • Intent to file
  • Accredited representation
  • Claim or appeal status
Boundary: This governed workflow does not determine eligibility, service connection, effective date, diagnostic code, or percentage evaluation. It does not create medical evidence. The veteran must verify all claim content against original records and current official requirements.
