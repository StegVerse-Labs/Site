Create your VA.gov sign-in

VA.gov → Sign In

Open VA.gov Sign In
Log in to VA.gov

Use one safe starting point: VA.gov

Open VA.gov Sign In. VA.gov will present the available Login.gov and ID.me choices. If you need a new account, create it from that VA.gov sign-in path.

Login.gov and ID.me are different account providers, but either can be used to sign in to VA.gov. You do not need to visit either provider first.

After you finish creating the account, return to VA.gov and sign in. Once you confirm both checkboxes above, DONE becomes available.
