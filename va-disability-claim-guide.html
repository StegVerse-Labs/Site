VA Claims Guide

Use this guide to gather records, organize evidence, and prepare a claim for your own review.

You stay in control. Nothing is filed or submitted for you.

Start with step 1Open VA Claims Chat

Phase 1 — Get access and preserve records

1Set up a secure VA.gov sign-in

Use Login.gov or ID.me. The guided workflow explains what you need and how to confirm that your account works.

Open the account setup steps

2Download official records

Download your available VA medical records and keep an untouched copy.

Open VA medical-record downloads

3Gather claim-related evidence

Collect service records, medical records, prior decisions, examination reports, witness statements, employment records, symptom notes, and exposure records that may affect the claim.

4Protect private information

Use a trusted device and network. Keep original records safe and share only what is needed.

Phase 2 — Build the evidence list

  • Current diagnosis or ongoing symptoms.
  • What happened during service.
  • When symptoms started and how they continued.
  • Medical opinions or test results.
  • How the condition affects daily life and work.
  • Statements from you or other people.
  • Conflicting records or missing information.

Keep documented facts separate from guesses or conclusions.

Phase 3 — Choose the claim type

  • Direct: the condition began in or resulted from service.
  • Secondary: another service-connected condition caused or worsened it.
  • Increase: an existing service-connected condition has worsened.
  • Presumptive or exposure: current VA rules may apply because of qualifying service.
  • Aggravation: service or a service-connected condition made it worse.

Phase 4 — Prepare materials to review

  • An evidence list for each condition.
  • A dated timeline with record references.
  • A draft personal statement using only confirmed facts.
  • A list of conflicting or unfavorable evidence.
  • A list of missing evidence and next steps.

Phase 5 — Review before filing

  1. Confirm your identity and account access.
  2. Review every important fact.
  3. Choose each condition you want to claim.
  4. Check current VA forms and requirements.
  5. Review the exact files you plan to submit.
  6. Sign and submit only when you are satisfied.
  7. Save the confirmation page and claim number.
