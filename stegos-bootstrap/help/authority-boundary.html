Authority boundary

This card identifies the authority planes used by the StegOS bootstrap. UI state, local persistence, copy controls, and page rendering never grant execution, custody, credential, publication, or activation authority.

Troubleshooting

If this card shows anything other than StegVerse activation authority, TV/TVC credential authority, and no required second non-StegVerse machine, treat the page as stale or invalid and do not continue state-changing work.
