    Confirmation rule

    A statement such as “done” does not automatically complete a card. Claims Chat asks about each required task and records completion only from the veteran’s confirmation.

    Document safety gate

    Private document upload and automated claim filing remain disabled on this public preview. Only use a secure upload control when the page explicitly presents one as active. The veteran retains claim-selection, fact-confirmation, signature, and submission authority.
