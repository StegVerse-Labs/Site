Ask the governed claims assistant

The current assistant provides official-source procedural and evidence guidance. Unsupported or ambiguous routes fail closed for review. It does not adjudicate, represent, diagnose, rate, sign, or file a claim.

VA Claims Chat: Ask about evidence requirements, direct or secondary service connection, filing stages, examinations, statements, or when accredited help is appropriate.

Every substantive future answer is required to separate source facts, veteran-record facts, assistant inference, contrary evidence, and unresolved uncertainty.

Private document workspace — coming next

Planned capabilities include secure PDF, image, and text upload; multi-file indexing; page-bound retrieval; evidence tables; timelines; contradiction detection; missing-evidence analysis; and exportable veteran-review packets.

Public upload remains disabled until substantive document execution, privacy controls, custody, reconstruction, deletion, and deployed-runtime receipts are verified.

Veteran-approved automated filing — future governed target

The intended path is automated preparation followed by explicit veteran review and authorization of the exact package. Filing will not occur from unreviewed uploads or inferred consent.

Non-authority boundary

StegVerse is not VA, an accredited representative, an attorney, or a clinician. The system may organize evidence and prepare drafts, but it may not invent facts, manufacture nexus evidence, target a percentage, or submit without explicit authority.
